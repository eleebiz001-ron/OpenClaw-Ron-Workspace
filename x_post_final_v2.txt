The tension between #Coinbase and traditional banks over interest on #Stablecoins is a defining moment for the #ClarityAct. 🏛️

Banks bear heavy compliance costs to maintain trust, while exchanges argue that 1:1 backed stablecoins aren't fractional reserves and shouldn't face identical restrictions. Both have valid points. However, unregulated high-yield competition risks "race to the bottom" scenarios, potentially leading to rug pulls and loss of principal for retail users.

We need a balanced compromise in the Clarity Act: oversight for consumer protection without banking monopolies.

Crucially, this must pass before May while Republicans hold the momentum. If we miss this window and Democrats win the election, the banking lobby will likely reclaim dominance. Who would have predicted the Democrats—once the party of labor—would align with legacy banks, while Republicans become the champions of digital freedom? 🇺🇸
